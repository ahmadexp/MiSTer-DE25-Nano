# DE25-Nano Si5332B clock access

## Confirmed hardware behavior

The DE25-Nano Rev. B routes three Si5332B outputs to FPGA clock inputs:

| Si5332B output | FPGA port | Agilex 5 pin | I/O standard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OUT1B | `CLOCK0_50` | DJ35 | 1.1 V |
| OUT0 | `CLOCK1_50` | V16 | 3.3 V LVCMOS |
| OUT1 | `CLOCK2_50` | BF23 | 3.3 V LVCMOS |

On 2026-08-23, a temporary PC110 image counted `CLOCK1_50` and
`CLOCK2_50` against `CLOCK0_50` in the live FPGA. Both measured 50,000 kHz.
The image passed the normal DE25 timing gate with 94 checks and no negative
slack. This proves that all three routed clock signals are active and usable
as FPGA reference clocks.

## Programming access boundary

The board's documented HPS and FPGA I2C buses do not include the Si5332B.
Read-only probes of the HPS I2C bus at the Si5332B default addresses `0x6a`
and `0x6b` both returned a remote-I/O error. The PCB instead exposes three
test pads beside U24 labeled `GND`, `SDA`, and `SCL`.

The U24 pads are now wired to FPGA GPIO: SDA to GPIO 1 (JP2) physical pin 1
(`BV14`) and SCL to GPIO 1 (JP2) physical pin 2 (`CG26`). Platform v2 uses a
strictly open-drain FPGA
I2C controller on these pins. Its first Menu image performs only address and
identity reads. Volatile writes remain disabled until the exact installed GM
variant and a matching complete ClockBuilder Pro register export are known.

The passive bridge PCB used for this connection is included under
[`hardware/de25-nano-bridge`](../hardware/de25-nano-bridge/README.md). Its
KiCad netlist confirms GPIO 1 pin 1 to SDA through a 1 kOhm series resistor and
GPIO 1 pin 2 to SCL through a second 1 kOhm series resistor. The GPIO 0 header
is a mechanical support only and has no electrical connections.

## Minimal enablement

Connect the U24 `GND`, `SDA`, and `SCL` pads to one of these interfaces:

1. A 3.3 V USB-to-I2C adapter left attached to the Quartus workstation.
2. An accessible 3.3 V HPS I2C header, after confirming pull-ups and that the
   address does not conflict with an existing peripheral.
3. Two suitable FPGA GPIO pins, with open-drain behavior and 3.3 V pull-ups.

The USB-to-I2C option is the least coupled to the MiSTer FPGA image and remains
available even when a failed core takes the fabric offline.

The live measurement implementation is in
`rtl/de25_clock_frequency_monitor.sv`; the JTAG reader is
`scripts/read-live-board-clocks.tcl`. The new control plane is under
`rtl/platform_v2/` and its design contract is documented in `PLATFORM_V2.md`.
